# Synthetic audit training case

**Fictional entity:** Al Nada General Trading (شركة الندى للتجارة العامة)  
**Period:** year ended 2025-12-31  
**Currency:** JOD  
**Data classification:** entirely synthetic training data; no real person, company, account, or transaction is represented.

## Files
- `trial-balance.csv`: simplified balanced trial balance (debits and credits each total JOD 118,000).
- `journal-entries.csv`: illustrative general-ledger entries, including a year-end item that should be investigated rather than presumed wrong.
- `bank-ledger.csv` and `bank-statement.csv`: simplified cash ledger and bank statement for reconciliation practice.
- `ar-aging.csv`: fictional accounts-receivable aging for follow-up practice.
- `inventory-count.csv`: book quantities and physical counts for investigation practice.

These are teaching fixtures, not a complete set of financial statements and not a real audit population. The files are intentionally small and do not fully cross-foot to every other file. Learners must identify source limitations and avoid treating them as a complete audit dataset. Do not import them into a client engagement or use them as evidence for a real opinion.
